# Power_BI_Dashboard_Superstore
The Superstore Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution that transforms transactional data into actionable insights. It analyzes sales, profit, products, categories, and regions through dynamic visualizations and filters, helping stakeholders identify trends, optimize performance, improve decision-making, and drive business growth.
The dashboard presents a comprehensive set of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to evaluate overall business performance. It includes **Total Sales**, **Total Profit of 175K**, which measures the overall profit earned by the company, **Average Order Value (AOV) of 228**, indicating the average amount spent by customers per order, and an **Average Profit Margin of 12%**, which reflects the efficiency of profitability. A **Monthly Sales Trend** line chart analyzes month-over-month sales performance and seasonal demand patterns, helping identify peak and low-performing periods for better strategic planning. The **Top 10 Products by Sales** table highlights the highest revenue-generating products, enabling management to recognize key sales drivers and focus on high-performing product lines. The **Region-wise Sales Analysis** bar chart compares sales performance across the West, East, Central, and South regions to identify strong markets and areas needing improvement. A **Sales by Category** donut chart illustrates the revenue contribution of Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture categories, providing insights into category performance and revenue distribution. The **Geographic Sales Distribution** map visualizes sales across various states and cities, supporting geographic performance analysis and identifying potential expansion opportunities. Additionally, interactive slicers allow users to filter data by region and product sub-category, making the dashboard highly dynamic and user-friendly. Overall, the dashboard answers critical business questions such as total sales and profits generated, top revenue-contributing products, best and worst-performing regions, and the product categories driving business growth.
